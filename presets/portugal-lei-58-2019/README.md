# Portugal — GDPR and Lei n.º 58/2019

A discovery policy that finds Portuguese personal data in a database and masks it, built for the
[General Data Protection Regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) — which
applies directly in Portugal — and
[Lei n.º 58/2019, de 8 de agosto](https://www.pgdlisboa.pt/leis/lei_mostra_articulado.php?nid=3118&tabela=leis),
which ensures its execution in Portuguese law and has not been amended since. The authority is the
**Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD)**.

Three things make this policy different from the others.

- **Portuguese law has crimes the Regulation does not.** Article 48.º of Lei n.º 58/2019, "Desvio
  de dados", punishes "quem copiar, subtrair, ceder ou transferir … dados pessoais sem previsão
  legal ou consentimento, independentemente da finalidade prosseguida" — copying personal data
  without a legal basis, whatever the purpose — and article 46.º their use against the purpose of
  collection; the penalty doubles for special categories. Article 29.º puts health data under
  "necessidade de conhecer" and requires that the data subject be **notified of any access**. For
  the public administration, Resolução do Conselho de Ministros n.º 41/2018 lists **"mascaramento,
  anonimização ou, sendo necessário, cifra"** among the requirements of the database layer.
- **The Constitution forbids a single national number** (art. 35.º, n.º 5), so the Cartão de
  Cidadão carries four numbers of four registers — civil identification, NIF, NISS and número de
  utente — that Lei n.º 7/2007 forbids interconnecting. Each is masked on its own mapping.
- **Every one of those numbers carries a check character, and this policy recomputes all of them**,
  with the IBAN/NIB, the prescription number and the CPE of an electricity or gas supply. Two fit the
  plugin's Check Digit as it is; the others are computed with lookup tables.

## Detection

Every data class has a **Path** classifier on the column name, in European Portuguese and in
English, and in the abbreviations of Portuguese systems (`CC`, `BI`, `NIC`, `NIF`, `NIPC`, `NISS`,
`NNU`, `CPE`, `DICOFRE`, `CP4`, `NUIPC`, `IRS`, `CRC`). Names that are ambiguous on their own (`NOME`,
`DOCUMENTO`, `PROCESSO`, `CONSULTA`) carry a lower strength and need the values to agree; generic
ones exclude what they are not (`NOME_PRODUTO`, `TIPO_DOCUMENTO`, and `NIF_EMPRESA`, which must not
read as a person's NIF; `SITUACAO_RESIDENCIA` is not an address, `COMPLEMENTO_DEPENDENCIA` not an
address detail).

Where the values have a recognizable shape or vocabulary, a **Regex** or **List** classifier finds
the data in columns whose name says nothing: document numbers of the Cartão de Cidadão, NIFs of
natural persons, NISS, IBANs (with the engine's MOD97 checksum) and NIBs, CPE, plates of the four
series, mobile and landline numbers, addresses by their type of street, postal codes `0000-000`,
cards (Luhn), IPs, MAC and IMEI, concelho and freguesia names, ethnic and religious vocabularies,
Portuguese parties and unions, ICD-10 and ICPC-2 codes, medicines, blood groups, health subsystems,
benefits such as the RSI, NUIPC numbers.

**Nine digits are ambiguous.** A NIF, a civil number with its check digit, a número de utente and a
landline share the shape, and the engine has no checksum for any of them. Without a column name, the
shape goes to the NIF; the número de utente is found by name only.

**Type** classifiers rule data classes out where the column cannot hold the data. **Padded values**
are handled as in every preset — see [`_lib/detect.mjs`](../_lib/detect.mjs).

## Masking

All algorithms are deterministic for the engine's key, so the same value masks the same way in every
table and every job. Placeholders such as `N/A` or `-` do not fail a row. Spaces around a value are
kept, and zeros on the left are not masked.

### Check characters

| Identifier | Rule | How | Table |
|---|---|---|---|
| Civil number (BI), NIF | 8 digits × 9…2 mod 11, 11 − r, 10 and 11 → 0 | Check Digit as it is | — |
| NISS | 10 digits × 29 23 19 17 13 11 7 5 3 2, 9 − (sum mod 10) | Check Digit, the leading 1 weighted 0 instead of 29 | — |
| Document number of the CC | Luhn over digits and letters (A = 10 … Z = 35) | state (sum, doubling) across 11 characters | 1,300 + 20 lines, 14 steps |
| Número de utente | ISO/IEC 7064 MOD 11,10 | state (1–10) | 130 + 10, 12 steps |
| Prescription number | ISO/IEC 7064 MOD 11-2, X for 10 | state (0–10) | 143 + 11, 18 steps |
| NIB (and so the IBAN) | 98 − (19 digits × 100 mod 97) | state (mod 97); the space an IBAN prints between the two digits is written back | 1,261 + 194, 24 steps |
| CPE, CUI | 16 digits mod 529, two letters of `TRWAGMYFPDXBNJZSQVHLCKE` (the Spanish CUPS rule) | state of three digits | 6,877 + 529, 22 steps |

The NISS trick: Check Digit writes ten minus the remainder, the NISS needs nine minus it; since only
natural persons' NISS — starting with 1 — are masked, giving that 1 the weight 0 (≡ 29 + 1 mod 10)
adds exactly the unit. Measured on the runner against bodies chosen for every remainder.

Every chain is run in `build.mjs` with the masking taken as the identity, and the build fails unless
a valid example comes back unchanged. The rules were checked against published values first: the
número de utente `629033874` (R. C. Teixeira, Universidade dos Açores, who found that the Portaria
n.º 981/95 says MOD 11-2 by mistake), an ORCID iD for MOD 11-2, the document numbers of python-stdnum
and django-localflavor (two independent implementations that agree), and two CPE published in
public tenders (`PT0002000066435112QY`, `PT0002000109439276TN`).

### L3, SAUDE, FIN, PENAL

- **L3** (art. 9 GDPR, art. 35.º, n.º 3 of the Constitution): ethnic origin (the ICOT 2023 groups),
  religion (the Censos 2021 question), philosophical beliefs, political opinion and party, trade
  union, biometric (art. 28.º, n.º 6: irreversible representations only) and genetic data (Lei n.º
  12/2005), sexual orientation and sex life, **gender identity** (Lei n.º 38/2018) and **victims of
  domestic violence** (Lei n.º 112/2009). Flags stay flags, codes become codes, values are replaced
  from the category's list or suppressed to `Sem informação`.
- **SAUDE** (art. 29.º of Lei n.º 58/2019, Lei n.º 12/2005): **número de utente** and **prescription
  number** with recomputed check characters, clinical and subsystem identifiers (ADSE, SAD, ADM,
  SAMS), subsystem, ICD-10-CM and ICPC-2 diagnoses, ICD-10-PCS procedures, medicines, clinical text,
  results, HIV and STIs, blood group, disability (Atestado Médico de Incapacidade Multiuso),
  dependency, occupational health (Lei n.º 102/2009), reproductive and mental health.
- **FIN** (art. 64.º of the Lei Geral Tributária): credit history (Central de Responsabilidades de
  Crédito, PERSI, insolvency), debt, income, assets, pension through the engine's CM Numeric, social
  benefits (RSI, abono, CSI, PSI, tarifa social) and tax data, whose sign and decimals stay.
- **PENAL** (art. 10 GDPR, art. 13.º b) of Lei n.º 58/2019, which counts contraordenações too):
  criminal record (Lei n.º 37/2015), the **certificate for work with minors** (Lei n.º 113/2009, Lei
  n.º 103/2015), the **NUIPC** and court case numbers (the number changes; year, control digit and
  code stay), and contraordenações (fines, licence points).

## Geography

The policy carries the 308 concelhos and the 3,092 freguesias of the Censos 2021 (INE indicator
0011609, read through the INE's JSON API; the freguesias add up to the 10,343,066 of the census), the
303 freguesias **Lei n.º 25-A/2025** brought back — effective when the bodies elected in October 2025
took office — with their DICOFRE codes and the population GeoNames gives them, and GeoNames centres
for the concelhos by DICO. **188 concelhos have fewer than 20,000 inhabitants** (1.6 million people)
and become the nearest concelho of at least 20,000 in the same distrito, or on the islands in the same
autonomous region — every distrito and both regions have one. **2,973 of the 3,092 freguesias** are
under 20,000 too (6.3 million people), and become the most populous freguesia still in existence of
the concelho their concelho goes to. Names come with and without accents, in capitals, and with the
distrito or the concelho beside them (`Mértola (Beja)`, `Ajuda, Lisboa`): 1,612 lines for concelhos,
29,506 for freguesias.

The **DICO** and **DICOFRE** follow the names (5,039 lines with the zero-free forms). Barcelos, with
more than 99 freguesias, has DICOFRE with letters (`0302FA`), matched before the numeric form. The
**CP4** follows the concelho with most addresses in the CTT postal code base (the 2020 copy of
`centraldedados/codigos_postais`): 253 of 760 are generalized.

## Limits

- **Masking with a key is pseudonymization.** Keep the key apart from the copy.
- **Freguesia populations** of 2025 are GeoNames', not the INE's; pre-2013 names that did not come
  back pass unchanged; `Madeirã` folds into `Madeira` and is generalized only with its concelho.
- **A CP4 may serve several concelhos**; the masked CP3 may not be assigned. The postal base is from
  2020.
- **Bank and branch are kept** in an account; the branch can tell a town.
- **The NUIPC control digit** has no public rule and is not recomputed.
- **Driving licence, residence permit and ADSE number** have no public format and are masked
  character by character.
- **A single `M` is read as masculino** (F/M); an H/M column needs its own algorithm.
- **Nine digits without a name go to the NIF**, whose masking keeps the first digit — wrong for a
  número de utente found that way.
- **Children**: a 60-day shift moves a few people across 13 (digital consent, art. 16.º) or 18.
- **Binary columns** (scanned cards — art. 5.º of Lei n.º 7/2007 —, photographs, PDFs) cannot be
  masked by these algorithms.

## Checking

```bash
node presets/portugal-lei-58-2019/build.mjs
node presets/portugal-lei-58-2019/verify.mjs
node presets/_doc/build.mjs portugal-lei-58-2019
```

`verify.mjs` reviews every classifier, profiles 153 columns (31 not personal data, 11 with opaque
names) with the whole policy and with the essential pack, and again padded with spaces and zeros;
audits the generalization of all concelhos, freguesias and CP4; masks every sample, placeholder and
algorithm sample input through the running app, plain and padded; and masks in bulk 200 document
numbers of the Cartão de Cidadão, 100 civil numbers, 200 NIFs, 150 NISS, 150 números de utente, 100
prescriptions, 200 IBANs (compact and printed), 100 NIBs and 100 CPE, checking that every output is
valid under its own rule, distinct, of the same shape, and keeps what it should keep. About nine
minutes.
